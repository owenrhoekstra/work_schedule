{% comment %} Identity edit form. Rendered on the employee details page (/details/). Posts `form=identity` to the details page URL. On success the view redirects to the reading page; on validation error it re-renders this page with bound errors. Footer variants: - No last_day: [Set Last Day] [Cancel] [Save details] - last_day set: [Cancel deactivation] [Cancel] [Save details] and a banner above the fields noting the scheduled deactivation. Save details always routes through _confirm_save_details.html (the submit interceptor in that modal preventDefaults and re-submits once confirmed). Cancel routes through _confirm_discard.html (unsaved edits warning). Set Last Day / Cancel deactivation buttons open their respective modals; both modals POST to their own endpoints (not to this form). `#cancel-deactivation-form` is a hidden sibling form (HTML forms cannot nest) that the cancel modal submits via JS. {% endcomment %}
